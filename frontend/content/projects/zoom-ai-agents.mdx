## The problem

The agency runs on Zoom. Advisors call leads on Zoom Phone, sales and onboarding happen in Zoom meetings, and client texts go out from Zoom numbers. An onboarding call holds most of what the agency needs to know about a new client: services, service areas, hours, the address. Those facts sat in recordings nobody had time to replay, and cloud recordings expire within days. A plain AI summary is no fix: a model can invent a detail or put the rep's words in the client's mouth. Calls also carry risk: clients read card numbers and passwords aloud, recording needs consent, and a text sent after a STOP is a real problem.

## What I built

- **Live transcripts over RTMS.** A Zoom Realtime Media Streams consumer opens a signed signaling socket and a transcripts-only media socket, and reconnects up to 3 times. Lines are stored with speaker and time after card numbers and passwords are redacted. Digits read out one group at a time are held until the reading ends, so no part of a card is stored.
- **Zoom Phone calls in the sales CRM.** A signed call-ended webhook wakes a 2-minute poll of Zoom's Call History. Each call is matched to a lead by its E.164 number, and Claude drafts a summary and a suggested disposition.
- **Connectors for clients' own Zoom Phone.** A client connects its account through a Server-to-Server OAuth app. Credentials are proven before they are saved: a token is minted, write scopes are refused and the users list must answer. A daily sync then copies calls, recordings and voicemails.
- **Meeting automation.** NestJS creates a client's Zoom room when a charge is approved, through a BullMQ queue with retries. Advisors book and move meetings on the lead owner's own Zoom user in an IANA timezone, and recurring schedules reach Google Calendar as RRULEs.
- **Texts from each advisor's own number.** Advisors connect their Zoom through user-managed OAuth with a signed state. A text counts as sent only after it reads back from the Zoom thread, and drafts written by a flow wait until a person presses Send.
- **A recording pipeline Claude can use.** Each day, meeting and phone recordings are copied to private S3, read-only on Zoom, then reindexed and matched to clients through aliases. 54 MCP tools let Claude search and sort recordings, manage the staff roster and contacts, and send texts.

Every path starts with a signed webhook, a live stream, or a poll that re-walks recent days so a late transcript still lands. Live lines are redacted as they are spoken, and every recording passes the card gate before it is stored. Agents read only redacted text, and a person confirms before a lead, order or client record changes.

## Call intelligence, checked against the recording

On an onboarding call, a map gives each of 150 profile fields exactly one owner. While the client talks, a coverage checker reads the new lines at most every 45 seconds and shows the rep which fields are still open. It is guidance only, writes nothing and has per-call caps on checks and cost.

After the call, the recording is the only source of answers. The model proposes a value for each field with the client's own quoted words, then code checks each quote, its timestamp and the speaker, and a geocoder verifies addresses. A field closes only on the client's own answer, so a single "yes" to a bundle of the rep's claims answers nothing. When every required field passes, the answers are written and read back. If any are missing, nothing is written and a follow-up call is prepared with only the open questions.

On sales calls, a deal review reads the redacted transcripts in one forced tool call, drops any quote that does not match word for word and computes the score in code, and the owner approves or holds the order. Each answer is stamped with the prompt version that produced it.

## More agents on Zoom

| Agent | What it does | Human step |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Call summary and disposition | Summarises each advisor call and suggests a disposition and callback number | The advisor taps Confirm |
| Recording classifier | Sorts recordings into internal, client, sales or external, with a time-aware staff roster | Unclear matches go to a review queue |
| Call intake reader | Reads each day's redacted client calls, meetings and texts for facts, requests and promises | A reviewer approves item by item |
| Call findings reader | Pulls short client quotes from calls for marketing content | Each finding waits for approval |

Claude Opus 5 runs the onboarding, review, intake and card frame checks, Claude Sonnet 4.5 writes summaries and findings, and the classifier runs in Claude through MCP tools.

## Built to be trusted

- **Consent first.** No part of an onboarding call after the opener starts until the rep confirms the recording disclosure or marks it declined. A declined call is never recorded or processed, and RTMS streams only the onboarding part, never the sales part or internal meetings.
- **Card data stopped at ingest.** Every recording is scanned before storage with a Luhn check and windows for expiry dates and security codes, and each redaction is logged. On a hit, audio is toned, video frames are boxed and re-checked, and nothing is released until a person resolves it. If the scanner is down, the recording is held.
- **Signed, idempotent webhooks.** Every receiver checks Zoom's HMAC-SHA256 signature, and meeting events carry a unique idempotency key, so a repeat is never processed twice. A stream is claimed in the database before it connects.
- **Guarded texting.** Every text passes the CRM opt-out flag, a STOP list, quiet hours on the recipient's clock and a rate limit. If consent cannot be read, nothing is sent.
- **Secrets stay out of chats.** Zoom user tokens are encrypted at rest and refresh tokens rotate. Client Zoom Phone secrets go in only through an admin route, never as a tool argument, and Google refresh tokens use AES-256-GCM.
