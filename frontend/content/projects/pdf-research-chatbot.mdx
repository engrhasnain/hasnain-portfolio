## The problem

Researchers and analysts spend hours searching through long PDFs for one number, one definition or one conclusion. Keyword search misses anything phrased differently, and general chatbots answer from memory instead of the actual documents.

## What I built

A question-and-answer app over your own PDFs, built on **retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)**.

- **Ingestion.** PDFs are parsed, cleaned and split into overlapping chunks so no idea is cut in half.
- **Embeddings and vector search.** Each chunk is embedded, and every question pulls back the passages that match its meaning, not just its keywords.
- **Grounded answers.** The model answers only from the retrieved passages. If the documents don't contain the answer, it says so.

- Questions that took a long search now take one message.
- Answers come from the documents, which makes them easy to check.
- The same pipeline works for contracts, manuals and policies, not only research papers.
