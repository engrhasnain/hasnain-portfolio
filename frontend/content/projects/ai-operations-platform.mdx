>

A digital marketing agency looks after many contractor businesses at once. Every one of them needs onboarding, content, reviews, social posts and calls handled, week after week.

All of that was **done by hand**, client by client. Every new client added more manual work for the same team.

The goal: **one platform where AI does the repetitive work**, a person approves anything that goes live, and Claude can work with the business through safe, typed tools.

</Problem>
